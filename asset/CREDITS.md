# Banner artwork credits

The lunar image embedded in `hero.svg` comes from **NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio**, [Moon Phase and Libration](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/5587/), named phase **waxing gibbous**.

- Visualizer: **Ernie Wright (USRA)**
- Planetary scientist: **Noah Petro (NASA/GSFC)**
- Lunar terrain data: **Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter / LOLA / LROC**
- [Original TIFF](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/vis/a000000/a005000/a005048/phase_waxing_gibbous.2158.tif)

The original frame was reduced to 640 × 640 pixels and encoded as a transparent PNG for the SVG. Its lunar features and lighting were not generated or retouched. The background, chart lines, typography, and grain are SVG elements. The handwritten signature is the existing `Handsig-white.png` asset.
